Fix the tax charge: apply the burden rate to taxable income, not gross

The defect recorded last round, now fixed. This was the tool's central calculation:
it looked a BURDEN rate up on taxable income and then multiplied it by GROSS income,
overcharging every household.

    tax            = rate(taxable) x taxable        (was: rate(taxable) x gross)
    effective_rate = tax / gross + social
    after_tax      = gross x (1 - effective_rate)   (unchanged, now a true inverse)

At CHF 100,000 gross, single, Bern -- taxable CHF 84,300, rate 15.76%:

    tax charged              CHF 15,758  ->  CHF 13,284
    tax share of gross          15.76%  ->     13.28%
    effective rate (incl 12.9%) 28.66%  ->     26.18%
    after-tax income         CHF 71,341  ->  CHF 73,816
    overcharge removed                     CHF 2,474

At CHF 70,000 gross the tax falls from CHF 9,314.51 to CHF 7,852.14 and after-tax
income rises from CHF 51,655.49 to CHF 53,117.86.

Why the rate is a burden rate, not a marginal one

The Bern table's own reference points prove it: 10.08% at CHF 40,000 means the burden
ON CHF 40,000 TAXABLE is CHF 4,032, and the model reproduces each published point
exactly -- pinned by `test_exact_official_rates_single` and
`bern_table_is_close_to_the_published_figures`. A rate of tax/taxable is a function
of taxable income, so applying it to gross is a category error.

What it changed, and why it got its own pass

`after_tax_income` feeds `monthly_after_tax`, which decides feasibility and the
optimal work percentage. So this moved recommendations, not just a displayed figure
-- which is why it was recorded last round rather than patched on the spot.

All 202 tests were re-run. Three failed, and all three were asserting the OLD
relation:

  test_total_rate_includes_social
  rate_accessors_agree_on_the_same_income
  optimizer::test_scenario_separates_tax_only_and_total_deductions

Each asserted `effective == tax_only + social`, which held only while the tax was
charged on gross -- the compensation cancelling out. They now assert the burden
decomposition AGAINST THE SCHEDULE rather than restated in the scenario's own terms,
so they cannot pass by both sides moving together:

    expected_effective = tax_only_rate * taxable / gross + social

Two invariants are now pinned explicitly, because they are what made the bug
invisible:

  - the tax share of gross must be STRICTLY BELOW the taxable rate (they were equal)
  - `after_tax_income == gross * (1 - effective_tax_rate)` exactly
