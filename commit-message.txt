Withdraw an untraceable figure from the deduction breakdown

`TaxDeductionBreakdown::non_deductible_total` was 2% of income after the deductible
items. It has no source, and nothing reads it -- it reached the user only through
`display.rs`, where it was printed between "Total deductible" and "Taxable income
after deductions" and therefore read as part of that arithmetic. It is not: taxable
income is gross minus `deductible_total` alone, so a reader could not reconcile the
lines and would reasonably conclude one of them was wrong.

FutureWork.md section 7 sets the standard that a number be traceable "to a data
source, to a named assumption, to a documented estimation method". This one is none
of the three, so it is withdrawn from the output rather than shown unexplained. The
struct field is retained for serialised compatibility, and
`non_deductible_total_feeds_no_tax_figure` pins that it stays out of every
calculation -- so if it ever gains a role, its removal from the display becomes a
real omission rather than a tidying-up.

The estimate's doc comment also no longer oversells it

It described itself as a "structured Swiss deduction model ... intended to represent
realistic, legally relevant deductions". Every component is hand-entered, and `rent`
at 12% of gross has no counterpart in Swiss tax law at all -- rent is not deductible
for an employee. The comment now says so, and names `src/deductions.rs` as the
sourced replacement.

One property of the estimate is now recorded because it is easy to misread from the
output: the 35% cap BINDS for families at moderate incomes. At CHF 60,000 with two
children both the plain and the `--family-tax-mode` figures are CHF 21,000 -- the
cap -- so the flag has no visible effect there, while it still works (+CHF 3,000)
wherever the cap does not bind. Measured across 20 family/income combinations before
concluding this was the cap behaving as designed rather than a defect.

Documentation

SWISS_TAX_DATA.md records both the withdrawn figure and the cap behaviour, with the
reasoning for each.
