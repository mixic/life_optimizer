Record an OPEN DEFECT: tax is charged on gross income, not taxable income

Found and verified this round, deliberately NOT fixed. It touches the tool's central
calculation and needs its own pass with the golden tests re-derived, not a patch in
passing. Recording it now so it cannot be lost.

The defect

`TaxSchedule` looks the burden rate up on TAXABLE income (as corrected in the
previous round) and then multiplies it by GROSS income:

    let taxable = self.taxable_income_after_estimated_deductions(gross);
    let base_tax_rate = self.lookup_tax_rate(taxable);   // rate IS a burden rate
    ...
    gross_income * (1.0 - (base_tax_rate + social))       // applied to GROSS

Why the rate is a burden rate

The Bern table's own reference points confirm it: 10.08% at CHF 40,000 means the
burden ON CHF 40,000 TAXABLE is CHF 4,032, and the model reproduces each published
point exactly -- which is what the published-reference test pins. A rate of
tax/taxable is a function of taxable income, so multiplying it by gross overcharges.

Size of the error: at CHF 100,000 gross, single, Bern, taxable is CHF 84,300 and the
rate 15.76%, giving CHF 15,758 charged against CHF 13,284 correct -- an overcharge of
CHF 2,474, about 19%.

`after_tax_income` was independently confirmed to consume exactly this quantity: at
CHF 70,000 it returns CHF 51,655.49, and 70000 * (1 - (0.1331 + 0.129)) gives the
same figure to the cent.

Why fixing it is not a one-liner

`after_tax_income` feeds `monthly_after_tax`, which decides FEASIBILITY and the
optimum work percentage. Correcting it will change which scenario the tool
recommends, not merely a displayed number.

A correction to the previous round's report

The "25.6% vs ~20%" claim compared the estimate's share of gross against the sourced
model's DEDUCTION share of gross -- two different quantities, so the comparison was
meaningless. Measured consistently across 20 household/income combinations, the
reported burden rate differs by 0.3 to 2.1 percentage points, and by 0.0pp once the
Bern table clamps at CHF 250,000. The corrected figure is also in the document.
