Let the CLI supply the household facts, and fix two insurance-premium bugs

Exposes the widened deduction engine through flags, so its property, pensioner and
insurance coverage is reachable rather than only testable -- and wiring the flags
found two more bugs. Test count rises from 194 to 198.

New flags on `optimize`

  --imputed-rental-value <CHF>   the home's Eigenmietwert
  --pensioner                    receives an AHV/IV pension
  --insurance-premiums <CHF>     declared premiums and savings interest

A fact that is NOT supplied is skipped rather than assumed, which is why an
unadorned run shows a small sourced total: it is reporting what it was told. The
"N further rule(s) were considered and not applied" line is the measure of what the
household did not declare. Supplying all three moves a CHF 100,000 married Zurich
household from CHF 3,000 to roughly CHF 14,000 of federal deductions.

Two bugs, found by running the flags rather than by reasoning

  The insurance-premium family deducted NOTHING, in all 27 jurisdictions

    These rules state only a CEILING -- `Betrag = 0`, `Prozent = 0`,
    `Maximum = 5800` -- so the generic `clamp(amount + percent * base, ...)`
    returned zero for every one of them. The deduction is what the taxpayer
    declared, capped at the canton's ceiling, which is the same shape as pillar 3a
    and needed the same treatment: ZH's married ceiling is 8,700, the federal one
    5,550, and a declared 3,000 deducts 3,000.

  A rule yielding zero was dropped SILENTLY

    This is why the first bug was invisible. The engine discarded zero-amount
    rules with a bare `continue`, so the family vanished from both the applied AND
    the skipped list -- and a dropped rule cannot be distinguished from a rule that
    does not exist. That is precisely the failure the `skipped` list was built to
    prevent, and it had a hole in it. A new test asserts rules are accounted for,
    so a future family cannot disappear the same way.

Refactor

The comparison function took eight arguments once the flags were added, so the
three facts are grouped into a `HouseholdFacts` struct with `Default`. The defaults
mean *not supplied*, which is the same distinction the engine makes, and it drops
the lint rather than silencing it.
