Price Basel-Landschaft from its legally-fixed Steuerfuss

Coverage rises from 22 to 23 of 26 cantons. BL's formula tariff already worked;
what was missing was its multiplier, and it turns out not to be missing at all.
Test count is unchanged at 186 -- one test was rewritten rather than added.

The workbook is blank for a structural reason, not a gap

The ESTV Steuerfuss workbook leaves the Liestal row ENTIRELY EMPTY for all 32
years it covers -- both the cantonal and the municipal cell -- with no footnote
explaining why. That is what kept BL unpriced after its tariff was implemented.

The reason is that BL does not adjust its multiplier annually. The
Steuerfussdekret (SGS 331.2) fixes the cantonal Steuerfuss at 100% of the normal
state tax, and when revenue has to change the canton amends the TARIFF BRACKETS
instead. That is entirely consistent with BL being the only canton in this
repository whose tariff is published as algebraic formulas rather than a band
table -- the two facts are the same fact.

The 100% figure is stated verbatim in a Landrat Vorlage restating the decree:
"Der kantonale Einkommenssteuerfuss fuer das Steuerjahr ... betraegt 100 Prozent
der normalen Staatsteuer vom Einkommen der natuerlichen Personen". Liestal's
municipal multiplier is 65%. BL at CHF 100,000 taxable now prices at 13.5%
effective (simple tax x 1.65).

Source strength is recorded per figure, because the two are not equal

  cantonal 100%   SGS 331.2, Dekret ueber den Steuerfuss, in force 01.01.2022
  municipal 65%   a cantonal tax comparison, NOT an ESTV publication

The Steuerfussdekret was supplied as a PDF. Its body text is image-only, so the
decree's IDENTITY is machine-verifiable from the file (the extractor recovers the
"Vademecum SGS 331.2" amendment table, the 16.12.2021 enactment date and the
"Gesetzessammlung Kanton Basel-Landschaft" signature) but its article text cannot
be quoted from it. The municipal figure is therefore the weakest of the four
numbers this entry carries, and its provenance says so -- so replacing it needs no
re-derivation of anything else.

BL used to be listed as a source exception and is deliberately removed from that
list. The list means "the source states no figure", not "this project has not
found one", and conflating the two is what let a determinate multiplier sit
unused. `source_exception_set_is_exactly_as_documented` now expects FR, GE, VS.

A finding about BL's married tariff, recorded rather than "fixed"

At CHF 100,000 the single and married cantonal figures are EQUAL. That is the
source's own shape, not a missing table: BL's married formulas differ from the
single ones only by an extra 0.49% band running from CHF 8,366 to the first shared
threshold at CHF 16,731. Above CHF 16,731 the two tables are the same expression,
so BL expresses no marital relief at this income.

Worth being explicit about the trap this avoided: the same export also declares
`Splittingfaktor 2.0`. Applying it would give 2 x simple(50,000) = 6,981.51
instead of 11,218.97 -- a materially different figure, and a marriage BONUS the
canton does not grant. Splitting is not applied because the export publishes
separate per-subject tables, and the test asserts that, so the two axes cannot
silently be combined.
